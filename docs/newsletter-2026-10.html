November — Breaking Barriers: Supporting Justice-Involved Job Seekers

Presented by Shannon McCracken, Career Services Consultant

Justice involvement can create significant and often misunderstood barriers to employment. Criminal and police records, gaps in employment, stigma, employer screening practices, conditions of release, and challenges related to housing, transportation, or community reintegration can all affect a job seeker's path forward. In this practical 60-minute Breaking Barriers session, employment service professionals will explore how to support justice-involved job seekers with confidence, dignity, and an employment-focused approach.

Participants will explore

  • Common employment barriers experienced by justice-involved job seekers
  • How stigma and assumptions can influence service delivery
  • How to have respectful conversations about justice involvement without turning an employment appointment into an interrogation
  • Criminal and police record checks and what employment professionals should understand
  • Supporting conversations about disclosure and employment gaps
  • Identifying realistic employment opportunities without unnecessarily limiting a job seeker's options
  • Preparing job seekers for interviews and employer questions
  • Building practical Employment Action Plans and connecting job seekers with appropriate community supports
  • Approaches to employer engagement that focus on skills, suitability, and opportunity

This session is designed for frontline employment professionals, case managers, employment advisors, job developers, and others supporting job seekers through Employment Ontario and community employment services.

Breaking barriers begins with seeing the job seeker before seeing the record.

Explore The Hub @PATH: Another Resource in Your Toolkit

Have you visited The Hub @PATH's refreshed website? Built with case managers and employment professionals in mind, it brings together practical resources and learning opportunities to support your day-to-day work with job seekers. Occupational Therapy Assessments

Are you or a job seeker you support unsure about their physical or cognitive abilities? Would you like more information on these factors to ensure the job seeker's path to employment is a good fit? Our Occupational Therapist can complete Functional Capacity Evaluations (FCEs) and cognitive assessments to provide you with more information — as well as consultations to assist job seekers in managing injury/illness symptoms, discuss accommodations, and more!
